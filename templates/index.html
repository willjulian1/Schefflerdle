How to Play

Guess the golfer in 6 tries or fewer. A new hint unlocks after every wrong guess.

  • Type a name and pick from the autocomplete list, or hit Skip if you're stuck — skipping counts as a try, same as a wrong guess.
  • Fewer guesses means a better score, using real golf scoring terms: Hole in One (1 guess), Eagle (2), Birdie (3), Par (4), Bogey (5), Double Bogey (6).
  • Use the major dropdown to only be quizzed on winners of a specific major, past or present.
  • Daily gives everyone the same golfer each day. Archive lets you replay any past day's puzzle — future days are locked so nothing gets spoiled.
